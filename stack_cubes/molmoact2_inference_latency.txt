MolmoAct2 Inference Latency — Stack Three Cubes
Model: subhodipsaha/molmoact2_stack_three_cubes (v1, 39 episodes)
Setup: Colab GPU → ngrok → laptop client at 25Hz

GPU     | Avg latency | Range
--------|-------------|----------
L4      | ~1085ms     | 1064–1155ms
A100    | ~990ms      | 978–1009ms

Improvement: ~90ms (~8%) — negligible in practice.

Conclusion: Bottleneck is NOT GPU memory bandwidth or FLOPS.
It is the flow matching denoising loop (sequential steps, hard to parallelize)
+ Molmo vision encoder tokenization. Both scale poorly with raw GPU upgrade.

The async chunk prefetch buffer (REFILL_THRESHOLD=28 steps) is the correct
architecture to absorb this latency — not a faster GPU.
